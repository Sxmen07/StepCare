How to get Family Care

Connecting with your family is simple, secure, and takes less than a minute.

1. Generate Code

The primary account holder logs in and generates a secure 6-character access code from the dashboard.

2. Share Securely

Send the unique code to a trusted family member via text, email, or in person. It expires in 24 hours.

3. Connect & View

Once entered, the accounts link up. Family members can now safely view upcoming appointments and care history.

What is StepCare?

StepCare is a premium home healthcare platform that bridges the gap between hospitals and your home. We dispatch certified Registered Nurses and Nurse Practitioners directly to your residence within 120 minutes.

Every visit integrates continuous vital telemetry and primary doctor reporting. We bring hospital-grade care to the comfort of your living room.

Why do you need Family Care?

  • Peace of Mind

    Monitor your elderly parents' or recovering relatives' medical visits without constantly calling them.

  • Coordinated Care

    Multiple family members can stay in the loop. No more miscommunication about who booked what appointment.

  • Emergency Readiness

    If a nurse flags an issue, the entire family circle is instantly aware and can take immediate action.
