Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. To ensure our nurses can provide the best care to all patients, we have a strict cancellation policy:

  • You may cancel your appointment at least 4 hours before the scheduled start time.
  • If your appointment is less than 4 hours away, the cancellation button will be disabled.
  • If you must cancel within 4 hours, please contact our support team directly.
Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee.
